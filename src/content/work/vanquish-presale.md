## The challenge

Launch a $1,000 trading course with only 20 seats. With so few places, every seat had to go to the right person, and the campaign had to build enough trust and urgency for people to pay $1,000 before the course had even started.

## What I did

I built one journey across four channels: social media, email, a landing page and a private Discord community. Each one handed people to the next, and the message, tone and timing stayed the same across all four.

The core of it was segmentation. The lead magnet told us each person's trading style, and their behaviour on email and the landing page told us how ready they were. Every email and call to action was written for that combination, rather than one message for the whole list.

## The A/B test

I split the audience at random through email automation and offered each half a different way to pay:

- **One-time payment** of $1,000 upfront.
- **Payment plan** split over several instalments.

The one-time group bought faster, which showed that urgency drives early sales. The payment plan reached slower decision-makers who might not have bought at all. Both groups contributed to the sell-out, and the result shaped pricing for future launches: lead with urgency, but always offer a flexible option.
