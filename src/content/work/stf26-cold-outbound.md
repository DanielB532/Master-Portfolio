## The challenge

Thousands of senior retail leaders who had never heard from us, a few weeks before the event. Sending the same promotion three times would have worn the list out after the first email.

## What I did

I wrote and built a three-email journey where each email had one job: name the problem, show the peers, then prove who would be in the room. They went out as plain-text, personal invitations from a director, with a single call to action in each, so they read like a note from a person rather than a newsletter.
