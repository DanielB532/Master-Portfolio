## The challenge

Future runs awards across dozens of magazine and trade brands, and each one needs entries before a fixed deadline. Every brand has its own reader and its own voice: an Ideal Home reader is not a pro AV integrator, and the emails had to sound right to both while still getting people to pay to enter.

## What I did

I wrote and planned entry campaigns across more than 20 UK and US brands. For each brand I defined who the reader was and rebuilt the messaging guidance around them, then segmented by audience type and how close each contact was to entering.

I led the copy and campaign planning for the Best of Show Awards at NAMM, building the full email journey before launch so the team wasn't writing under deadline pressure. For the NAMM project I also used Apollo to build targeted prospect lists and shaped the outreach messaging, which supported a 20% revenue increase.
