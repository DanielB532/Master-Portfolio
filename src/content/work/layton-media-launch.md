## The challenge

When I joined, there was a list of about 14,000 emails and nothing else: no campaigns, no strategy and no product to sell yet. The goal was to build a full funnel from scratch and launch an online course in January.

## What I did

I planned the funnel in three phases: build up and nurture, launch, then retention. I wrote the social video scripts that fed the list, wrote the emails, set up ActiveCampaign with segmentation, and tracked opens, clicks, conversions, unsubscribes and revenue throughout.

## How I wrote it

- **Short paragraphs.** Three lines at most, so nobody gives up halfway down.
- **Disrupt, intrigue, click.** A subject line that stops the scroll, a story that keeps them reading, and one clear call to action.
- **Easy reading level.** Written for a 5th to 6th grade reading age, so anyone can take it in fast.
- **Problem, then solution.** Name the reader's problem first, then show the course as the way out.

## What I tested

- Generic subject lines against emotional ones.
- Different calls to action and language styles across social and email.
- Layouts, including where images sit and how the copy is structured.
- Canadian English against British English, which made no real difference.
