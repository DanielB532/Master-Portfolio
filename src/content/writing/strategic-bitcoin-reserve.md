President Trump has signed an Executive Order to create a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, marking a major shift in how the U.S. government handles digital assets. This decision cements Bitcoin's role in the economy and ensures that the U.S. benefits from its long-term value.

## What is the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve?

The Strategic Bitcoin Reserve will hold Bitcoin that the government has obtained through criminal or civil asset forfeiture cases, meaning taxpayers will not have to pay for it. Instead, the government will keep and manage Bitcoin that has already been seized, rather than selling it.

Previously, the government sold off seized Bitcoin, leading to losses of over $17 billion in potential value. Now, these assets will be held as a store of value, similar to how gold is kept in Fort Knox.

## How much Bitcoin does the U.S. government own?

It is estimated that the government holds around 200,000 Bitcoin, but there has never been a full audit of its digital assets. As part of the Executive Order, a full review of all government-held Bitcoin will be conducted to get an accurate total.

## No more premature Bitcoin sales

A key part of this plan is that the government will no longer sell off its Bitcoin holdings. Instead, Bitcoin in the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve will be managed carefully to maximise its value for the country.

The Treasury and Commerce Departments will also explore ways to acquire more Bitcoin without increasing costs for taxpayers.

## The U.S. Digital Asset Stockpile

In addition to Bitcoin, the Executive Order creates a U.S. Digital Asset Stockpile. This will hold other digital assets (like Ethereum, XRP, Solana and Cardano) that the government acquires through forfeiture cases.

However, unlike Bitcoin, the government will not actively buy more of these assets. It will only manage those it already owns. The Treasury Department will oversee this Stockpile to ensure these assets are handled responsibly.

## A pro-crypto future for the U.S.

This move shows that the U.S. is fully embracing digital assets and positioning itself as a leader in the crypto space. President Trump has repeatedly expressed his support for Bitcoin, recently saying: "I also love Bitcoin."

By protecting and growing the nation's digital assets, this policy aims to make the U.S. the "Crypto capital of the world."

With clear strategies now in place, the government is taking a long-term approach to Bitcoin and digital assets, ensuring they are used to benefit the country instead of being sold too early.

This Executive Order marks a historic step toward integrating cryptocurrency into the U.S. financial system and securing its place in the global economy.
