In recent months, Donald Trump has emerged as the pro-crypto candidate for the U.S. presidency, making big promises to change how the country manages digital assets. Now, with Trump winning the election, the world of crypto could see major changes under his leadership. Here, we look at some of Trump's key promises and how they might shape the future of cryptocurrency in America.

## 1. Pro-crypto stance and market reactions

Donald Trump has aligned himself with the crypto industry in a way few politicians have. He's promised to support the industry, bringing in significant campaign funding from major figures in the sector.

On Election Night, as his victory became clear, Bitcoin surged to an all-time high of over $75,000, and if Trump's policies come to life, many experts believe Bitcoin could hit $100,000 before he's even sworn in. This optimism has boosted shares in crypto-linked companies like Coinbase and MicroStrategy, suggesting that Trump's presidency could fuel even more market growth.

## 2. A strategic national crypto stockpile

One of Trump's standout promises is to create a national crypto reserve. He's pledged that his administration will hold on to all Bitcoin currently owned by the government. This is a break from current policy, where the U.S. Marshals Service auctions off seized Bitcoin, impacting market prices.

By keeping this "strategic crypto stockpile", Trump believes he can strengthen the U.S. economy and give the government a financial asset that could grow in value. This policy could stabilise crypto markets by preventing sudden sales that often push prices down. Trump's approach suggests he sees Bitcoin as a valuable national resource and wants to retain it to benefit future generations.

## 3. Replacing SEC Chair Gary Gensler

Trump has made it clear that he isn't a fan of the current U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Chair, Gary Gensler. Trump has promised that, when elected, he will replace Gensler, whom he blames for excessive regulation on crypto companies. Gensler has taken more than 100 legal actions against crypto firms, leading many in the industry to feel that his leadership stifles innovation.

While Trump doesn't have the direct power to fire Gensler, he can appoint a new chair who would likely be more supportive of the crypto sector. Trump has also pledged to create a "bitcoin and crypto presidential advisory council", which would shape regulations to be more supportive of the industry.

## 4. All Bitcoin mining in America

In an attempt to make the U.S. the leading force in global crypto, Trump has proposed that all remaining Bitcoin should be mined in America. He met with key industry figures to discuss how U.S.-based Bitcoin mining could support "energy dominance" and give the country an edge over international rivals like China and Russia.

Trump believes that this strategy would also benefit America's energy sector by increasing demand for electricity. By focusing on domestic mining, Trump hopes to create jobs, boost energy production and secure a critical industry for the future.

## 5. Interest rate cuts and crypto growth

Trump has promised to cut interest rates, which could have a positive effect on the crypto market. Lower rates tend to make it easier for people and businesses to borrow money, which can increase spending and investment. Historically, these conditions have led to rising crypto prices, as investors are more willing to put money into high-growth assets like Bitcoin.

Although the Federal Reserve, which sets interest rates, operates independently from the White House, Trump's commitment to rate cuts aligns with his pro-crypto economic stance. Many in the crypto industry see this as a favourable move that would encourage growth in the sector.

## Final thoughts

Donald Trump's promises could represent a major shift for the crypto industry in the U.S. His plans to create a national crypto reserve, replace the SEC chair, boost domestic Bitcoin mining and encourage growth through rate cuts all suggest he wants America to be at the forefront of the digital asset world.

While these policies may be bold, they reflect a strong commitment to supporting the future of cryptocurrency. If Trump follows through on his promises, America could emerge as a leader in the global crypto economy, providing new opportunities for growth and innovation in the sector.
