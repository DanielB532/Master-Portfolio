The Stargate Project is a new, exciting plan to create some of the most advanced artificial intelligence (AI) systems in the world. Over the next four years, it will invest a massive $500 billion to build new technology infrastructure in the United States. This work starts now, with $100 billion being spent immediately.

This project isn't just about AI. It's about creating jobs for hundreds of thousands of Americans, boosting the US economy and helping the country become a leader in this important field.

## What is the Stargate Project trying to do?

The goal of the Stargate Project is to make the United States the best place in the world for AI technology. It will create the tools and systems needed to improve how AI works and to ensure this technology can benefit everyone, not just a few people.

The project will play an important role in national security. By leading in AI, the US will have better tools to protect itself and its allies from threats around the world.

## Who is behind the project?

Some of the biggest companies in technology and finance are working together on the Stargate Project:

- SoftBank is in charge of the money.
- OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, will handle the technical side.

Other key companies involved include Oracle, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Arm. These businesses will work together to build and operate the systems needed to create better AI tools.

Masayoshi Son, the leader of SoftBank, will be the chairman of the project.

## Where is the project happening?

Work has already started in Texas and the team is looking for more locations across the US to build additional facilities. These facilities will create thousands of new jobs and help support local economies.

## Why does this matter?

AI is becoming a big part of our everyday lives; it helps with things like online searches, customer service and even medical research. The Stargate Project wants to take this to the next level by creating even smarter AI systems that can solve big challenges, such as climate change and disease.

By doing this, the project hopes to make life better for everyone, not just in the US but across the world.

## A team effort

This project builds on strong partnerships:

- OpenAI and NVIDIA have been working together since 2016.
- OpenAI and Oracle will deepen their partnership to improve the technology.
- Microsoft's Azure will continue to be a key platform for OpenAI as it develops its systems.

These companies are combining their strengths to create something that no one could achieve alone.

## Looking ahead

The Stargate Project has big goals, but at its heart, it's about helping people. By building better AI, the team hopes to empower creative thinkers, solve tough problems and make life better for all of us.

This is just the beginning of an exciting journey, and the Stargate Project is determined to make a positive difference for the US and the world.
